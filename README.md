# OVRPlayer

Play your standalone Meta Quest games on a PC VR headset.

OVRPlayer runs Quest games on Windows and shows them in the headset connected
to your PC, with your controllers, as if they were PC VR games.

## What it does

- **A launcher you use in VR**: your games as a grid, started with a click of
  the trigger.
- **A built-in store**: sign in with your Meta account, browse the Quest
  catalogue, and install the games you own directly from Meta.
- **Certified titles**: games we have patched, tuned and verified carry a
  green check. Others can be installed anyway, at your own risk.

## Status

Early and experimental. A hobby project, non-commercial and free.

You need to own the games you play: OVRPlayer installs them from your own Meta
account and does not provide any game.
